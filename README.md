# HR Analytics SQL Portfolio Project

## Project Overview

This project analyzes employee data using Microsoft SQL Server to identify workforce patterns, employee attrition trends, and important HR insights.

The project contains 50 SQL business questions covering employee demographics, attrition, job roles, compensation, work experience, job satisfaction, overtime, and other HR-related factors.

## Business Objective

The main objectives of this project are:

- Analyze employee workforce distribution
- Understand employee attrition patterns
- Identify factors associated with employee attrition
- Analyze employee compensation and experience
- Explore job satisfaction and workplace factors
- Generate useful HR business insights using SQL

## Dataset

** Dataset:** IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance

**Records:** 1,470 employees

**Columns:** 35

The dataset contains employee information such as department, job role, age, income, overtime, job satisfaction, work experience, and attrition status.

## Key Insights

Some important findings from the analysis include:

- Overall employee attrition rate is **16.12%**.
- Sales has an attrition rate of **20.63%**.
- Employees working overtime have an attrition rate of **30.53%**, compared with **10.44%** for employees who do not work overtime.
- Sales Representatives have an attrition rate of **39.76%**.
- Employees aged under 25 have an attrition rate of **39.18%**.
- Employees with 0–2 years at the company have an attrition rate of **29.82%**.
- Job Level 1 has an attrition rate of **26.34%**.
- Employees with the lowest job involvement level have an attrition rate of **33.73%**.

These findings describe associations observed in this dataset and should not be interpreted as proof of causation.
